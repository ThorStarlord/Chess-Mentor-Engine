# Participant and game-set criteria

Status: Frozen with protocol version 1.0

## Intended participant

Recruit approximately 6-8 regular online players rated about 1400-1800 in rapid or classical-strength games. The sample should include players who actively want to improve and who already have some experience with engine review.

## Include when the participant can

- provide 10-30 recent games;
- identify the time control and approximate date range;
- explain ordinary engine-review terms such as evaluation, blunder, and best move;
- complete a short comparison interview or questionnaire;
- discuss what they would practice next.

## Exclude or flag when

- the available games are mostly bullet and would obscure deliberate decision processes;
- fewer than 10 usable games are available;
- games are not comparable because of missing move data or material corruption;
- a known unusual event materially affected the games, such as a long interruption or deliberate experiment;
- the participant cannot review both reports independently.

An exclusion or flag is a study-record decision, not a judgment about the player. Record it in the manifest or analysis notes.

## Frozen game set

Choose the set before analysis. Record:

- game-set ID;
- game count;
- source and format;
- date range;
- time controls;
- side or color information when relevant;
- exclusions and reasons;
- any optional player goal or self-report supplied before analysis.

Use one fixed set for both conditions. Do not add a game because it supports a diagnosis.
