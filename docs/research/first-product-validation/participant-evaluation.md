# Participant evaluation

Status: Questionnaire for protocol version 1.0

Show the two reports as Analysis A and Analysis B. Record which report is shown first. Do not reveal the report conditions until all comparison responses are complete.

Use this scale for rating questions:

```text
1 = not at all
2 = slightly
3 = moderately
4 = strongly
5 = extremely
```

## Immediate understanding

1. In your own words, what did Analysis A say was happening in your games?
2. In your own words, what did Analysis B say was happening in your games?
3. Which claims in either report felt like direct observations, and which felt like interpretations?

## Rating questions

Rate each analysis separately from 1 to 5.

| Statement | Analysis A | Analysis B |
| --- | --- | --- |
| This analysis explains a recurring weakness in my play. |  |  |

## Comparison questions

1. Which analysis better explains what repeatedly goes wrong in your games? Why?
2. Which analysis gives you a clearer idea of what to practice next? Why?
3. Which analysis is more actionable? What would you do differently in your next practice session?
4. Which claims do you trust most?
5. Which claims seem speculative or unsupported?
6. Did either analysis change your understanding of your chess? If so, how?
7. Did either analysis identify something you had not noticed before?
8. Did either analysis merely restate engine mistakes in more elaborate language?
9. Which analysis feels more specific to your games? Could either report have been given to many players at your rating?
10. If you had only one of these analyses to decide how to spend your next three hours of chess practice, which would you choose and why?

## Credibility check

1. Point to one example where the evidence clearly supports the report's conclusion.
2. Point to one example that could support a different explanation.
3. What additional information would you need before accepting the main recommendation?

## Final disclosure

After the comparison responses are recorded, disclose which analysis was the conventional review and which was the recurring-pattern diagnostic. Ask:

1. Does knowing the purpose of the reports change your evaluation?
2. What would make this kind of report more useful or more trustworthy?
