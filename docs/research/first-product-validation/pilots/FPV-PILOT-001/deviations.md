# FPV-PILOT-001 deviations

Status: Recorded before participant evaluation.

## D-001. Pre-evaluation exposure

Before participant evaluation, P01 was exposed outside the repository to a summary containing enough information to potentially identify the recurring-pattern analysis. The exposure included the selected recurring-pattern diagnosis, supporting evidence, diagnostic interpretation, competing explanations, and proposed training intervention.

Blinding status: compromised before participant evaluation.

The exposure makes condition content recognizable, but it does not establish that P01 knows the randomized participant-facing A/B label mapping. The randomized order is therefore retained to reduce presentation-order effects, but the evaluation cannot support a clean blinded-condition preference inference.

### Claim limitation

P01's later responses remain usable for perceived usefulness, explanatory value, actionability, evidence credibility, trust, novelty, specificity, qualitative criticism, and reported training preference.

They must not be used as clean evidence for blinded condition preference or for a claim that P01 blindly preferred the Chess Mentor analysis.

### Operational lesson

Future execution reports must not expose diagnostic content, condition identity, or recognizable summaries before participant evaluation. This is a candidate future protocol improvement, not a change to the frozen main protocol or this pilot protocol.
