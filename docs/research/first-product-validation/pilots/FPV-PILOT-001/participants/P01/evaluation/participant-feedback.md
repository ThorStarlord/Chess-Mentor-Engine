## REAL PARTICIPANT QUALITATIVE FEEDBACK

P01 identified the central problem as:

P01's concern is that the analysis may understand chess concepts abstractly but may not have enough representation of the actual board state to explain why a move was good or bad. P01 questioned whether played move, engine evaluation delta, best move, and candidate principal variations provide enough spatial and positional context for model-assisted pedagogical reasoning.

P01 also supplied this example position:


The example played move was `22...g6??`. The supplied Lichess annotation indicated that `22...Qe7` was best. P01's point was not only that the move was objectively wrong, but that a report containing only move labels, evaluations, and principal variations may require too much board reconstruction to make the explanation useful.
