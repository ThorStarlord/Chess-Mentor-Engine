# FPV-PILOT-001 pilot protocol

Status: Frozen before substantive P01 diagnosis.

Protocol version: 1.0

## Purpose

Test whether the recurring-pattern diagnostic method is operationally and epistemically useful on one real participant's heterogeneous 10+0 game history.

## Dataset

One participant, P01, supplied one local PGN file containing the participant's 10+0 games. The dataset includes human and AI opposition, rated and casual games, Standard games, and From Position games.

## Main methodological deviation

Opponent and position-context heterogeneity is allowed for this pilot. The main frozen protocol's stronger interpretation of a clean sample of ordinary competitive human rapid games remains unchanged and is not replaced by this pilot.

## Required stratification

Evidence must be separated at minimum into:

- Human Standard;
- AI Standard;
- AI From Position.

Additional dimensions may be used when they clarify the evidence, including rated versus casual, earlier versus more recent, result, color, and termination.

## Pilot claim

Given a heterogeneous but time-control-consistent sample of one player's 10+0 games, Chess Mentor Engine can identify at least one candidate recurring decision pattern that is traceable to multiple positions, survives contradictory-evidence review, and yields a useful training hypothesis.

This is exploratory and participant-specific. It does not establish generalization to competitive human rapid, a proven cognitive cause, or product validation across the target segment.

## Analysis rules

Use the same frozen P01 game set for both comparison conditions. Preserve the layers of objective evidence, observable behavior, diagnostic hypothesis, and intervention. Select at most one primary candidate pattern. Return insufficient evidence if the chain cannot be defended.

Use the embedded Lichess analysis annotations as an experimental evidence source where cited. Record that engine name, version, settings, and limits are unknown unless independently supplied. Do not present the annotations as evidence of what P01 thought.

## Failure conditions

The pilot counts against the mechanism if no defensible recurring pattern can be found, apparent recurrence exists only because unlike contexts were pooled, the diagnostic collapses into generic advice, explanations exceed their evidence, contradictory cases substantially defeat the pattern, the intervention does not follow from the evidence, competing explanations cannot be distinguished, or the report becomes persuasive storytelling rather than traceable inference.

## Evaluation boundary

Participant evaluation must use blinded Analysis A and Analysis B and must not be fabricated. A reviewer assessment must remain separate from participant preference. This pilot cannot establish rating improvement, transfer, mastery, automated diagnosis reliability, a production learner model, or the main experiment's result.
