# Pilot 004 longitudinal design

## Planned batches

Three sequential independent batches are planned, each containing 8–10 fresh positions. The minimum is 8 and the maximum is 10; the batch size cannot be chosen after seeing responses.

- Batch 1: generate candidate participant-specific hypotheses.
- Batch 2: test candidate hypotheses and contradictory cases.
- Batch 3: test persistence, contradiction, and context dependence.

## Independence

Every batch requires a new exposure audit. A position is not fresh if its answer was exposed through Pilots 001–003, an earlier Pilot 004 batch, engine analysis, or prior research discussion. Instrument awareness is allowed; position-answer exposure is not.

New games may replenish the eligible pool only after provenance is recorded, the candidate pool is frozen, private analysis is performed, and exposure is audited.

## Continuation

Continue to the next planned batch only when the current batch is complete, evidence remains interpretable, burden is acceptable, and fresh positions remain available. Carry hypotheses forward as candidates, not conclusions.

## Stopping

Stop after the planned batches, if fresh supply is insufficient, burden is excessive, evidence is demand-characteristic dominated, no interpretable recurrence emerges, probing adds negligible information, or protocol integrity is compromised.
