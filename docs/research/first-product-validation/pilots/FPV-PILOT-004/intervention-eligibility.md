# Pilot 004 intervention eligibility

A recurring hypothesis becomes eligible for a later training intervention only when recurrence is supported across independent positions, contradictory cases have been reviewed, the discrepancy is pedagogically actionable, alternative explanations do not defeat it, and P01-specific relevance is strong enough.

Diagnosis supported does not mean intervention effective. Any intervention must be tested in a later phase with a new fresh-position batch. The possible extension is:

`supported hypothesis → targeted intervention → fresh batch → recurrence/change measurement`
