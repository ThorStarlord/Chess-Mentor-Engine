# Pilot 004 evaluation plan

For every position compare A1, A2, and objective evidence. Record what was noticed, what appeared only after probing, decision changes, probe effects, objective chess consequences, position-level discrepancies, controls, and remaining unknowns.

Each batch records positions analyzed, evidence completeness, probe effects, discrepancies, controls, candidate and retired hypotheses, contradictions, uncertainties, burden, and demand-characteristic concerns.

Success means repeated traceable within-participant evidence that distinguishes correct reasoning, local discrepancies, and candidate recurrence while preserving uncertainty and contradictory evidence. It does not mean finding a weakness.

Track burden descriptively: completion burden, repetition, fatigue, mechanical probing, and whether probing changes normal thinking. This is research UX evidence, not medical or cognitive measurement.

Readiness for later multi-participant work requires interpretable A1/A2 separation, useful but manageable probing, preserved raw evidence, working controls, detectable contamination, acceptable burden, and preventable position-answer leakage. Do not assume readiness is met.
