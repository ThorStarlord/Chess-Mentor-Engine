Status: DESIGNED AND FROZEN — NOT EXECUTED.

Title: Instrument-Aware N-of-1 Longitudinal Calibration  
Participant: P01 only

## Relationship to Pilot 003

Pilot 003 remains the stronger clean instrument-calibration experiment and is blocked on an unexposed P02. Pilot 004 is a separate executable N-of-1 product-discovery study under a weaker claim ceiling. It does not supersede or represent successful Pilot 003 execution.

## Research question

Within one known, instrument-aware participant, can standardized diagnostic probing add traceable reasoning evidence beyond a minimal initial response, reveal position-level Reasoning Discrepancies, and support participant-specific hypotheses that recur across independent fresh-position batches?

Secondary question: Does diagnostic probing change P01's move, candidate set, interpretation, or confidence before engine feedback?

## Sequence

`A0 Position Context → A1 Minimal-Prompt Response → freeze → A2 Standardized Diagnostic Probe → freeze → E Engine/objective evidence → analyst comparison`

P01 must not see engine answers before A1 and A2 are frozen. Instrument awareness is intentional in this study, not a deviation; demand-characteristic risk is higher than in a clean study.

## Claim ceiling

The study cannot establish probe-naive cognition, unexposed-player behavior, typical participant behavior, population categories, cross-player generalization, automated diagnosis reliability, rating improvement, transfer, production learner-model validity, production architecture, or causal cognitive traits. It also cannot cleanly estimate how instrument knowledge changes P01's minimal response.

## Outcomes

Permitted overall outcomes: `Promising`, `Mixed`, `Insufficient Evidence`, or `Demand-Characteristic Dominated`.

## Execution boundary

This protocol freezes design only. It does not select Batch 1 positions, collect responses, reveal engine evidence, test an intervention, or implement product features.
