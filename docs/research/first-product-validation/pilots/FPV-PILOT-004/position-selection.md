# Pilot 004 position selection

Select 8–10 positions per batch only after exposure auditing. Selection must be frozen before P01 response collection and must not optimize for confirmation of an emerging hypothesis.

Seek a balanced mixture where the available history supports it: tactical opportunity, tactical defense, strategic planning, quiet improvement, opponent-oriented decisions, successful/control decisions, and ambiguous decisions. Do not manufacture categories or equate informativeness with largest engine loss.

Include correct or successful cases. The participant must not know internal classes. Record FEN, move provenance, exposure class, selection rationale, and eligibility internally.

Use `FRESH`, `EXPOSED`, or `UNCERTAIN`. Only FRESH positions provide clean within-Pilot-004 comparisons. Do not consume positions in this design task.
