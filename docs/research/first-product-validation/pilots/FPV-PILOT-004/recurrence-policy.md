# Pilot 004 recurrence policy

Classify a discrepancy as `isolated`, `candidate recurrence`, `supported recurrence`, `contradicted`, or `insufficient`.

Recurrence requires more than one independent position, traceable common structure, comparable reasoning evidence, review of controls and contradictory cases, and consideration of alternatives. “Feels similar” is insufficient.

Batch 1 may generate candidates; later batches must test them rather than merely elaborate them. Actively seek support, contradiction, successful counterexamples, and context-specific exceptions. Allow hypotheses to disappear.

Candidate participant-specific hypotheses must list supporting evidence, contradictory evidence, context, competing explanations, evidence strength, and claim ceiling.
