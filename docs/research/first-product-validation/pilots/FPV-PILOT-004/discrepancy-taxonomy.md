# Pilot 004 discrepancy taxonomy

## Definition

Reasoning Discrepancy means: a traceable mismatch between P01's reported pre-engine reasoning and an objectively relevant demand or consequence of the position.

Candidate codes, allowing multiple or none: critical feature not reported; strong candidate not generated; opponent resource not anticipated; continuation calculated incorrectly; resulting position evaluated incorrectly; strategic target identified but no executable move generated; move correct but rationale incomplete; correct reasoning / no discrepancy; unclear; OTHER / EMERGENT.

These are research codes, not production learner categories. A position-level discrepancy is not a recurring pattern, learner hypothesis, confirmed weakness, or causal cognitive trait.
