# P01 decision reconstruction

Pilot: FPV-PILOT-002  
Participant: P01  
Stage: Pre-engine decision evidence

Please analyze each position independently and record your genuine current reasoning before any engine, external analysis, prior report, or additional commentary. Do not try to infer why a position was selected or whether it is expected to be easy or difficult.

For each position, record the moves you seriously considered, your choice, your reasoning, the strongest reply you expect, your expected continuation, your plan or objective, your confidence from 1 to 5, and whether your reasoning is primarily tactical, strategic, defensive, or other.

If you remember the original game, keep that recollection separate from your current position-based reasoning. Complete the pre-engine reasoning first, and consult the original game result only afterward if practical.
