# Pilot 002 participant questioning

Status: Question set frozen. P01 has not been asked these questions.

Ask the questions before revealing engine evaluation, best move, principal variation, or Pilot 001 diagnosis where practical. Record answers as `PLAYER DECISION EVIDENCE`, not objective chess evidence.

## Pre-engine questions

For each selected position, ask:

1. What moves did you seriously consider here?
2. Why did you choose the move you played?
3. What did you think your opponent's strongest reply was?
4. What position did you expect after your intended continuation?
5. Were you solving a tactical problem, following a plan, or reacting quickly?
6. How confident were you in the move, from 1 to 5?
7. Were you aware of the quieter alternative, if one is later shown?

## Post-engine questions

After the pre-engine answers are frozen, reveal the relevant engine evidence and ask:

1. Does the analysis change your understanding of the position?
2. Which spatial relationship or threat was easiest or hardest to see?
3. Did the preferred move address something you had considered?
4. Does the explanation distinguish between not seeing a reply and mis-evaluating a reply?
5. What additional context would make the explanation trustworthy?

## Recording rules

Preserve pre-engine and post-engine responses separately. Do not rewrite uncertainty into a clean narrative. Record pauses, uncertainty, disagreement, and answers that contradict the proposed diagnosis.
