# Pilot 002 position selection

Status: Selection rules frozen. No positions selected yet.

Select 5-8 positions from P01-GS01. Do not reanalyze all 30 games initially.

## Required coverage

The set should include:

- positions previously considered as support for the Pilot 001 candidate pattern;
- at least one human Standard position where possible;
- at least one AI Standard or AI From Position position;
- at least one contradictory or ambiguous case;
- more than one game;
- more than one type of decision error where the evidence permits.

Include at least one negative control where P01 made a good move or handled a superficially similar situation correctly.

## Selection procedure

1. List all Pilot 001 candidate supporting and contradictory positions.
2. Group them by game, stratum, decision type, and date.
3. Remove duplicates and positions whose evidence cannot be reconstructed.
4. Select a bounded set that covers support, contradiction, and context variation.
5. Record rejected eligible positions and the reason for rejection.
6. Freeze the final set before asking P01 any decision-reconstruction questions.

Do not select only positions that support the preferred hypothesis. Do not add a position because P01 gives a compelling answer to another position.
