# Pilot 003 reasoning instrument

## Evidence states

- A0 — frozen position state and provenance.
- A1 — raw Stage A spontaneous response, frozen before probing.
- A2 — raw Stage B response to the standardized probe, frozen before engine reveal.
- E — objective chess and engine evidence, revealed after A1 and A2.

## Stage A prompt

> What do you think about this position, and what would you play?

The facilitator must not add chess heuristics, candidate-count requirements, calculation requirements, or hints about the selected position.

## Stage B prompt set

Ask the same six questions, in the same order, for every position:

1. What other moves did you seriously consider?
2. What do you think your opponent's strongest reply to your chosen move is?
3. What continuation do you expect after that reply?
4. What are you trying to accomplish with your move?
5. Is there anything about the position that makes you uncertain?
6. How confident are you in your choice, from 1 to 5?

Record responses verbatim. Do not improvise follow-ups during the clean run.

## Decision-change record

After A2, record only explicit evidence of whether the move, candidate set, expected reply, intended plan, or confidence changed. A changed answer remains valid evidence and is not treated as contamination by itself.
