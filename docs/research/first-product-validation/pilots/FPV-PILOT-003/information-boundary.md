# FPV-PILOT-003 information boundary

## Internal Research View

May contain the full protocol, hypotheses, later-stage instrument, exposure classifications, control/error labels, engine evidence, selection rationale, contamination analysis, and condition mapping.

## Participant View

Before spontaneous reasoning is frozen, may contain only the stage-appropriate position packet and the frozen Stage A prompt. It must not expose later-stage instrument content, hypotheses that predict the instrument, internal classifications, selection rationale, engine answers, or expected discrepancies.

## Surfaces covered

The boundary applies to files, filenames, headings, generated summaries, chat/status reports, and operator instructions. The research operator may have the Internal Research View; the participant receives only the Participant View.

## Safe pre-A1 status

`P02 STAGE A PACKAGE READY — AWAITING SPONTANEOUS PARTICIPANT RESPONSES`

This status must not be accompanied by later-stage questions, engine conclusions, selection rationale, or control/error classifications.
