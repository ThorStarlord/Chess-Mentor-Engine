# Pilot 003 position selection

Select 6–10 positions only after execution begins and exposure auditing is complete.

## Eligibility

Each candidate must be classified before participant use as `FRESH`, `EXPOSED`, or `UNCERTAIN`. Only FRESH positions are eligible for clean A1 → A2 evidence. Pilot 002 positions whose engine evidence has now been revealed are not fresh for P01.

Do not disclose engine evaluations, best moves, labels, rankings, PVs, internal roles, or selection rationale to the participant.

## Balance

Seek a small balanced set containing, where the audited data supports it:

- tactical opportunity;
- tactical defense;
- strategic planning;
- quiet positional improvement;
- prophylactic or opponent-oriented decisions;
- successful/control decisions.

Do not manufacture categories or weaken exposure rules to fill a quota.

## Controls

Include historical moves expected to be successful under the frozen selection rules. Controls test whether sparse responding is general or decision-specific. The participant must not know control status.

## Selection record

The later execution record must include candidate provenance, FEN, game/move reference, exposure classification, class rationale, and condition eligibility. This design intentionally consumes no fresh positions.
