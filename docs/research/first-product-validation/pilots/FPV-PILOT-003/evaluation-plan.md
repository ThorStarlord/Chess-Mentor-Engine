# Pilot 003 evaluation plan

## Per-position comparison

After A1, A2, and E are frozen, document:

- information available from Stage A;
- new information introduced by Stage B;
- whether the move, candidates, reply, plan, or confidence changed;
- whether Stage B distinguished competing explanations;
- whether Stage B appeared instructional;
- objective evidence and remaining unknowns.

Compare Stage A → Stage B → objective evidence. Do not collapse the dimensions into one score without justification.

## Hypothesis assessment

Assess H1–H5 against all positions, including controls. H5 and Instrument Contamination remain legitimate outcomes. A changed move is not automatically positive or negative.

## Outcome categories

Use one conservative category: `Promising`, `Mixed`, `Insufficient Evidence`, or `Instrument Contamination`.

## Replication readiness

Do not scale until the pilot provides evidence that Stage A captures spontaneous reasoning, Stage B adds meaningful information, wording is sufficiently neutral, contamination is detectable, raw/coded evidence remain separate, discrepancies are interpretable, burden is acceptable, controls work, and fresh-position leakage can be prevented. These are provisional criteria, not assumed successes.
