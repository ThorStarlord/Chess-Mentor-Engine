# Pilot 003 probe design

## Design intent

Stage B is diagnostic probing: standardized questions intended to surface reasoning that Stage A did not expose. It is not a chess lesson and must occur before engine reveal.

## Neutrality criteria

A probe should be standardized, understandable, position-independent, non-leading, minimally instructional, reproducible, and capable of surfacing reasoning without naming a chess solution method.

## Frozen probe set

The six questions in [reasoning-instrument.md](reasoning-instrument.md) are the complete Stage B probe set. No question may be added, removed, or reworded position by position during execution.

## Probe-effect variables

Compare A1 with A2 for:

- move retained or changed;
- candidate set unchanged or expanded/changed;
- expected reply newly supplied or changed;
- intended plan newly supplied or changed;
- confidence newly supplied or changed;
- explicit evidence of new understanding.

These changes are observations, not automatic evidence of improvement.
