# Synthesis plan

Status: Frozen with protocol version 1.0

Do not calculate one weighted success score. Keep participant usefulness, reviewer evidence quality, and analyst agreement visible as separate findings.

## Summarize by participant

For each participant, record:

- game count and evidence horizon;
- whether the report produced insufficient evidence, a candidate pattern, a supported pattern, or a strongly supported pattern;
- Analysis A and B ratings for usefulness, actionability, explanatory value, trust, novelty, and specificity;
- preferred analysis for choosing what to practice and the reason;
- whether the participant could restate the pattern;
- whether the participant distinguished evidence from interpretation;
- objections and competing explanations raised by the participant.

## Summarize across the study

Describe:

- participant-level A/B preferences;
- usefulness and actionability;
- explanatory power and novelty;
- trust and specificity;
- reviewer evidence-quality judgments;
- analyst disagreement;
- insufficient-evidence rate;
- recurring failure modes;
- whether value required 20-30 games rather than approximately 10.

Use quotations only with participant permission and an anonymous ID. Do not present small-sample counts as population estimates.

## Proceed to domain modeling

Recommend proceeding when several participants receive reports that reviewers judge well-supported, participants can distinguish evidence from hypothesis, the diagnostic output changes the stated next practice priority, and the result is meaningfully more specific than conventional review. No single metric is sufficient.

## Refine and repeat

Recommend refinement when the reports contain useful recurring observations but causal explanations, interventions, evidence selection, or reviewer agreement remain weak. Examples include value only with 20-30 games, good diagnosis but poor intervention, or participant value with inconsistent support.

## Reconsider the thesis

Recommend reconsideration when reports mostly restate engine analysis, generic advice is as useful as the diagnosis, participants cannot trust or understand the evidence chain, independent analysts cannot reach compatible patterns, or persistence does not appear to add value.

## Harmful result

Escalate a harmful failure mode if reports sound personal and psychologically persuasive while reviewers find weak support. Do not treat participant preference as a reason to proceed in that case.
