# Strategy

Satisfy a `product_strategy` responsibility by producing `strategy_doc`.

## Inputs

Require product vision/direction, target segment, problem/opportunity context, and relevant evidence. Prefer durable discovery, market, hypothesis, product, and owner-intent artifacts.

## Procedure

1. Read [references/contract.md](references/contract.md).
2. State the desired future and current strategic context without inventing TAM, baselines, or commitments.
3. Define a focused target segment plus explicit non-targets.
4. Connect evidence-backed problems to strategic choices and deliberate non-choices.
5. Describe differentiation and monetization as supported conclusions or hypotheses according to evidence strength.
6. Define proposed outcome measures and roadmap themes without converting them into observed results or committed dates.
7. Surface consequential assumptions, risks, dependencies, and decisions requiring owner authority.
8. Return a `proposed` strategy artifact and control.

## Stop or downgrade

- If customer/market evidence is weak, state that weakness in the strategy rather than calling the problem validated.
- Unknown pricing, resource, financial, or timeline facts remain unknown/proposed.
- Do not mark strategy approved or ratified without explicit durable authority evidence.

## Boundary

`strategy recommendation != ratified strategy`, `theme != delivery commitment`, and `metric target != observed result`.
