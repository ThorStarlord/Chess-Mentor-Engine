# Hypothesis methodology

Adapted from `lucasgaravelli/pm-skills-claude-code/.claude/commands/hypothesis.md` at commit `21cbb2903d740d10fc65c667aea97d3ee8657349` (MIT, Flowgrammers 2026).

Prefer a falsifiable belief structure: `We believe [intervention] for [specific target] will cause [expected user behavior/outcome], observed through [primary measure], under [decision horizon/threshold]`.

The exact sentence may vary, but the proposition must permit a result that causes rejection or revision. Define target segment/context, problem/opportunity and evidence, expected behavior/outcome, primary measure, optional secondary measure, guardrail measure, value/usability/viability/feasibility assumptions, validation method, and success/pivot/kill criteria.

Thresholds may be owner-supplied, measured from evidence, or explicitly proposed by the agent. Keep those sources distinguishable.
