# thesis-review-packet

Use only when a Level-3 decision cannot be resolved without reviewing a
governing Level-4 commitment.

Produce:

`artifacts/thesis_review_packet.md`

## Procedure

1. Identify the exact governing commitment and authority reference.
2. State the thesis tension/challenge.
3. Ground it in decision-relevant evidence.
4. Identify current work that depends on the challenged commitment.
5. Present materially plausible Level-4 dispositions from:
   `REAFFIRM | REINTERPRET | REVISE | RETIRE | SUPERSEDE`.
6. State downstream Level-3 reconciliation required after ratification.
7. Preserve that the packet cannot ratify a thesis transition itself.

```text
thesis tension != thesis outcome
packet != ratification
Level 3 != Level 4 authority
```
