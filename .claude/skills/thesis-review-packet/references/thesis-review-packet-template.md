# Thesis Review Packet

## 1. Challenged Commitment

Identify the exact Level-4 commitment and authority reference.

## 2. Thesis Tension

Explain the contradiction or pressure.

## 3. Evidence

List decision-relevant evidence.

## 4. Affected Level-3 Work

Identify responsibilities/paths whose warrant depends on the commitment.

## 5. Candidate Level-4 Dispositions

Use only `REAFFIRM | REINTERPRET | REVISE | RETIRE | SUPERSEDE`.

## 6. Downstream Reconciliation

State what Level 3 must reassess after ratification.

## 7. Authority Boundary

State that owner ratification remains required.
