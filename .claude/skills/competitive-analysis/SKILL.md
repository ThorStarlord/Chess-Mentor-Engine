# Competitive analysis

Satisfy a `market_understanding` responsibility by producing `market_analysis`.

## Inputs

Require the product/context, target segment, and named competitors or alternatives. Prefer current first-party competitor sources, customer evidence, and market evidence when available.

## Procedure

1. Read [references/contract.md](references/contract.md).
2. Establish an evidence cutoff and source ledger before comparing claims.
3. Compare only dimensions relevant to the target segment and decision.
4. Separate source-backed competitor facts from interpretation and unknowns.
5. Identify product strengths, weaknesses, market gaps, and threats with evidence status.
6. Draft positioning and differentiation options as recommendations, not observed truths.
7. Surface stale, conflicting, or absent market evidence instead of filling gaps from memory.
8. Return `market_analysis` and control to the active agent.

## Stop or downgrade

- If current competitor evidence cannot be obtained, produce a partial snapshot and list missing checks.
- Treat roadmap rumors, hiring signals, and inferred strategy as inference unless verified.
- Do not manufacture percentages, pricing, timelines, or market share.
- Recommendations do not authorize roadmap or pricing changes.

## Boundary

`market snapshot != market truth`, `feature comparison != strategic priority`, and `positioning recommendation != owner decision`.
