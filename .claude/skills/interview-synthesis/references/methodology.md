# Interview synthesis methodology

Adapted from `lucasgaravelli/pm-skills-claude-code/.claude/commands/interview-synthesis.md` at commit `21cbb2903d740d10fc65c667aea97d3ee8657349` (MIT, Flowgrammers 2026).

First analyze each supplied source independently: identify relevant JTBD, pains/gains, behaviors, alternatives, triggers, and exact customer statements where useful. Preserve source identity for every material observation.

Then synthesize across the supplied corpus: cluster repeated themes; report actual frequency as `n/N` only when N is known from the supplied corpus; preserve contradictory evidence rather than averaging it away; distinguish measured/reported frequency from agent-estimated severity; and identify implications and next research separately from facts/findings.

A recommendation may cite a finding, but recommendation strength should remain proportional to the evidence. Never turn a plausible pattern into a quoted or counted observation unless it is present in the source set.
