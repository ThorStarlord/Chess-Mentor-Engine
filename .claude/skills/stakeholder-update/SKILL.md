# Stakeholder update

Satisfy a `communication` responsibility by producing `stakeholder_update`.

## Inputs

Require the audience, purpose, as-of boundary, and source material. In Campaign context, prefer durable Campaign state, admitted evidence, transitions, decisions, handoff, and target snapshot over remembered chat. Accept product/metric/risk evidence outside Campaign when explicitly supplied.

## Procedure

1. Read [references/contract.md](references/contract.md).
2. Bind audience, purpose (`inform` or `decision_request`), as-of boundary, and source scope.
3. Inventory claims before writing the narrative. Preserve observed, derived, inferred, hypothesized, ratified, and unresolved distinctions where consequential.
4. Describe completed work only from durable completion evidence; plans and tickets remain intent/work-in-progress evidence.
5. Report ratified decisions only with an authority source. Proposed options/recommendations remain proposed.
6. State metrics with their source/currentness and avoid turning movement into causal explanation without evidence.
7. Present risks, implications, and decision requests concisely while preserving uncertainty and counter-evidence.
8. Express next steps with proposed owner roles/timing unless explicit authority establishes an assignment or commitment.
9. Draft the communication without sending, posting, emailing, or updating external stakeholder systems.
10. Stop when the bounded audience can understand current state, evidence, decision needs, and next actions without material distortion.
11. Return `stakeholder_update` and control.

## Boundary

`reported status != independently verified current state`, `plan != completion`, `inference != ratified decision`, `proposed owner != assignment`, `draft != sent update`, and `clear narrative != stronger evidence`.
